How it works

No changes to your agent's code. Countersign speaks MCP, so any agent that uses tools through MCP works with it.

  1. Point your agent at Countersign

    Replace your MCP server URL with acme.dp.countersign.dev/crm. Your agent only sees the tools it's allowed to use.

  2. Your rules decide every call

    Who the agent is, who it acts for, and what it's trying to do are checked against your rules in milliseconds. Risky calls wait for a person; dangerous ones are blocked with a reason the agent understands.

  3. Every call gets a signed receipt

    Each decision is recorded in a chain of signed receipts. Your security team, a customer or an auditor can verify them with a public key, without trusting us.

What you get

Rules for every action

Allow, block, limit or require approval by agent role, tool, data type, amount, time and environment. Ready-made rule packs for production safety and personal data.

Approvals where people already work

Risky actions go to Slack or email with everything the approver needs. Approvals are tied to the exact request, so nothing can change after someone says yes.

Sensitive data stays protected

Card numbers, government IDs and personal details are masked in arguments and results. Data read from customers can't be sent outside the company by a hijacked agent.

Proof that stands on its own

Signed, chained receipts and a decision explainer show exactly why each call was allowed, held or blocked. Export evidence for security reviews and audits.
